How to play

  • ✥Drag anywhere to run. A joystick appears under your thumb, and up is always toward the ball
  • ⚡Tap (or put down a second finger) to jump and dash at the ball. Touch it within half a second for a SMASH: a hard shot aimed at the goal
  • ◉Score in the bot's magenta goal. Guard your own cyan goal behind you
  • ⏱2-minute match. Tied at full time? Golden goal: next goal wins
  • ★Points: goal 100, smash goal 150, save 30, win +250
